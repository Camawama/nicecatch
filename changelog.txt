## [Unreleased]

### Fixed (big-pass follow-up)
- **Fish tanks actually connect now**: tanks placed together merge into one aquarium —
  shared glass walls and interior water seams vanish, so a 2x2x2 or 3x3x3 reads as a
  single glass box of water. The occupant SWIMS the whole connected build on a slow
  wandering path, facing along its motion, upright (no more sideways spinning) and
  finning at real-time speed (the display's animation clock was frozen and its "dry fish"
  pose flopped it on its side — both fixed). Bigger builds genuinely hold bigger fish.
  Only LIVE fish go in: a stamped caught fish, a fish bucket, or a fish spawn egg —
  loot-table fillets are dinner, not pets.
- **Rod stands are usable**: right-clicking a stand with a cast rod now actually parks it
  (the reel-control interception let the click through to blocks it belongs to), the
  weird floating rod arm is gone from the ITEM model, an attached bell now renders as a
  small bell on the stand and can be snipped off with shears, and a parked line survives
  its owner logging out (frozen until they return).
- **Water murk v2**: smoothly interpolated noise (no more blocky patches, seamless across
  chunks), and murk now scales with DEPTH — shore shallows stay readable, the drop-off
  past them goes dark. Exactly like standing over real water.
- **Bubble jet**: reach doubled (12 default, up to 32), intake bubbles now drift into the
  soul-sand sides so the pump visibly breathes in and out, and jets idle for free when no
  player is near.
- **Fish schools stopped pitching in unison**: each fish now smooths its swim pitch at
  its own rate and carries its own quiet undulation, so a school reads as individuals in
  the same current instead of one animation stamped eight times.
- **No cut-line rod cooldown in creative.**
- **Fish Trap and Rod Stand moved to the Functional Blocks creative tab.**
- **Old worlds finally get retuned defaults**: config files now carry a version stamp,
  and values still sitting on a superseded default are migrated automatically on load
  (anything you customized is never touched). Applies retroactively to every default
  retuned so far (bite chance, dart window, net cooldown, school spawn weight, the
  difficulty-ladder threshold, tension tuning).
- Plus the rest of REVIEW_NOTES.md: chain-bite tension grace, client crank cap honoring
  crankOverdrive, tamed pets exempt from aggressive fish, stale-yaw fish jumps, dead
  config/code removed, vibration and bubble-jet cost caps, weight/trait cache work, and
  a network protocol version bump.

### Added (the big pass)
- **Bubble Jet block**: a horizontal bubble column. Submerged, it blows a sideways
  current of bubbles through open water (config `blocks.bubbleJetRange`/`bubbleJetPush`),
  pushing items, fish, boats, and swimmers along the stream. Crafted from iron, soul
  sand, and magma.
- **Fish Tank block**: a glass display case. Right-click with a caught fish to set it
  swimming inside — the real fish model at the caught individual's stamped size (fitted
  to the glass), slowly finning — and right-click empty-handed to take it back, weight
  and traits intact. For the trophy too good to eat.
- **Rod Stand block**: cast a line, right-click the stand with the rod to park it — the
  line STAYS OUT with nobody holding it, and your hands are free to cast another rod.
  Manage a whole row of lines. Parked lines attract and hook fish on their own (patient
  bite window, `blocks.standBiteWindowTicks`); the bobber churns the whole time, and a
  BELL right-clicked onto the stand rings out when something takes it. Grabbing the rod
  with a fish on sets the hook instantly — the fight starts on the spot. The line renders
  from the stand, not from your empty hands.
- **Patchy water murk** (`client.waterMurk*`): the water surface darkens in irregular
  noise patches, so fish under a murky patch are hard to spot from above and a bite gets
  its surprise back. Underwater visibility is untouched. (Baked into chunk geometry;
  shader packs that replace the water renderer bypass it.)
- **Fish jump** (`jumps:true` in fishProfiles — salmon and tropical fish by default,
  `species.fishJumpChance`): idle near-surface fish occasionally leap clear of the water.
- **Weather matters** (`species.rainBiteMultiplier` / `thunderBiteMultiplier`): rain on
  the water puts fish on the feed; a thunderstorm sends them deep and off it (they also
  idle in the bottom band until it passes).
- **Heavyweights hold the deep** (`species.deepWaterPreferenceLbs`): fish past the
  threshold idle in the deepest water available, and the surface clamp now pushes
  proportionally harder the further a body pokes out — no more trophy backs cruising in
  open air.
- **Aggressive fish** (`aggressive:true` in fishProfiles — sharks, pike, thrashers by
  default): predators with the flag attack ANY creature swimming in their water that is
  small enough to be prey by hitbox ratio. A rabbit paddling over a big pike is having a
  bad day. Non-fish prey takes real damage instead of being swallowed; players are exempt
  unless `species.aggressiveAttackPlayers` says otherwise.
- **Moving bait** (`lure_movement` in fishProfiles, `species.lureMovementBonus`): species
  that hunt movement strike a drifting or slowly-reeled bobber far more readily than a
  dead-still one — and a slow reel-in no longer stops the line attracting at all, so you
  can genuinely WORK the lure. Defaults researched per species: pike/tuna/salmon chase
  drift hard, cod and pufferfish prefer it still.
- **Six fishing-rod enchantments** (enchanting table + anvil, vanilla and Aquaculture
  rods alike): Quick Reel I-III (+10%/lvl reel speed everywhere), Long Cast I-III
  (+15%/lvl cast power), Iron Line I-II (+25%/lvl tension tolerance), Sure Hook I-II
  (+30%/lvl bite window), Alluring I-II (wider attraction radius), and Auto Attract I
  (the bobber twitches itself like a worked lure — pairs with moving-bait species).
- **Supplemental spawning respects `doMobSpawning`** (also in this pass, see below).

### Fixed (the big pass)
- **The robotic synchronized bobber dance is gone.** Fish aimed their feet-origin at a
  fixed depth below the bobber, so a big fish's body breached, the surface clamp shoved
  it down, the approach pushed it back up — and every schoolmate, hitting the same
  surface at the same math, bobbed in perfect eerie unison. All bobber-approach depths
  are now measured for the fish's BODY (its top stays under), bite reach scales with
  body size so big mouths can still reach the hook, and orbit speeds get a per-fish
  offset so schools drift out of phase.

### Balance (multiplayer playtest feedback)
- **The difficulty ladder — every demand a fish can make is now weight-gated.**
  Under 5 lbs (`fight.soundPhaseMinWeightLbs`): a pure basic reel — the fish NEVER runs
  at all (runs are what take line, and a pan fish takes no line): no hook-set run, no
  lifts, no dives, no charges, no darts. It thrashes and holds while the crank brings it
  straight home in a few seconds. From 5 lbs: runs begin, plus Sounding dives that demand
  rod lifts. From 25 lbs (`fight.chargePhaseMinWeightLbs`): the Charge ("reel fast, take
  up the slack!"). From 75 lbs (`fight.dartMinWeightLbs`): PULL LEFT/RIGHT darts.
  Beginners land pan fish on reeling alone; every extra mechanic is something the fish
  earned by being huge. Weak fish also barely resist the winch during runs (run reel
  effectiveness scales with strength), and cranking against a small fish's run puts far
  less strain on the line (run tension scales with strength too).
  NOTE for existing worlds: soundPhaseMinWeightLbs may be stored at its old default 25 —
  set it to 5 in the world's serverconfig toml for the intended tiers.
- **No more instant red tension on a big bite.** Tension gains ramp in from zero over the
  first ~2 seconds of a fight (`tension.earlyTensionGraceTicks`), so a heavyweight's
  hook-set run can't max the bar before anyone has read the situation.
- **Darts must be answered with an EASED pull, not a jerk.** Only a smooth, sustained
  sideways sweep counts (`fight.dartMaxPullPerTick` caps progress per tick), and yanking
  violently past the cap strains the line (`fight.dartJerkTensionPenalty`). Stop the reel,
  then lean into the pull.
- **Heavyweights can actually be beaten now.** A big fish used to rip line to the end of
  the spool faster than any counter existed and escape — cranking harder did nothing (by
  design: you can't out-crank a run) but nothing else worked either. Three fixes: the
  drag now loads up with line out (`fight.runDragTaper` — at the end of the spool a run
  keeps only ~40% of its force, so monsters stall further and further out instead of
  spooling you), lifting the rod resists boring runs again (the "pull the rod up" hint
  now genuinely works on every run style, not just diving fish — ease off the crank and
  lift), and sprinting against the loaded drag burns extra stamina scaled by line taken
  (`fight.dragRunFatigue`) — a monster's own spectacular runs are what finally wear it
  down. The rhythm: let it run while lifting, then crank hard between runs.
- **Reeling faster finally matters** (`fight.crankOverdrive`, default 1.5): crank speed
  used to hit a hard ceiling at the base full-crank rate, so furious circling pulled no
  harder than a lazy circle — exactly why a strong fish felt uncounterable. Circling
  faster than the base rate now keeps adding pull, up to 1.5x, at the cost of
  proportionally more line tension. Base resistance also eased: a fresh monster now
  cancels 40% of your reel speed instead of 50% (`fight.reelResistance`).
- **Pull up and HOLD.** Rod lift is now a held position, not a continuous motion: one
  upward pull raises the rod and keeping the mouse still keeps it raised (it eases down
  only very slowly; push the mouse down to lower it deliberately, release the grip to
  drop it). No more pump-reposition-pump running off the mousepad — and since the level
  survives cranking, "lift, then reel" against a sounding fish finally works as one
  motion. The rod stays visibly raised in hand while held high.
- **The end of the spool is a hard brake**: the drag now bites from two blocks before
  the line's end (stronger than the old at-the-line damp), so a run bleeds to a crawl at
  the wall — an actively fighting angler (rod high, cranking between runs) can hold a
  monster there and work it back, and escape takes sustained uncountered pull.
- **The tension corridor on big fish is livable now.** Cranking hard through a CALM phase
  — the exact play the fight design demands — used to red-line the bar in ~5 seconds
  against a monster, leaving no speed that both kept up with the fish and kept the line
  alive. Calm-crank tension now scales gentler with strength (sustained overdrive
  cranking on a monster roughly breaks even with recovery), run-crank tension eased
  (0.30 to 0.24 per rev — still absolutely a way to snap the line), and tension bleeds
  faster while bracing (0.012 to 0.016/tick): a braced run clears a full red bar in ~3
  seconds. The rhythm is unchanged, just survivable: brace + lift through runs, crank
  flat-out between them.
- **Fish swim-sound volume config actually works now.** Vanilla plays fish swim sounds
  through the positional sound path, and the mod was listening on the entity path — the
  handler never fired, so `fishSwimSoundVolume` did nothing. It now hooks the base sound
  event and matches the fish-swim sound itself.
- **Heavy-carry rendering fixes**: while both arms heft a heavy fish, the other hand's
  item no longer renders at the same time (no torch floating over the cradling arm — it
  reappears when the fish is put away), and a heavy fish held in the OFF hand is no
  longer upside down (the mirrored yaw inverted under the third-person frame correction;
  both hands now share one upright orientation).
- **The Charge finally works like the hint says.** Two fixes: (1) the line is SLACK while
  a fish charges you, so cranking through a charge no longer builds tension — it bleeds
  off fast instead (crank flat out, free; the only sin during a charge is not reeling);
  (2) the gained ground must actually be reeled up: sustain a modest crank through the
  charge (`fight.chargeSlackCrankPerTick`) or the fish spits the slack at the phase's end
  and bolts right back out with it — a charge you don't reel through was just the fish
  resting. Merely watching it swim closer (or only lifting the rod) no longer counts.
- **Trait system: stale-roll bug fixed** (also the `/nicecatch summon ... none` ghost
  traits). Trait modifiers were cached per entity on first lookup — which happens in the
  entity CONSTRUCTOR, before the real UUID is set (loading, spawn packets, and summon all
  set it afterward) — freezing a random placeholder roll in as the fish's traits. The
  cache now revalidates against the entity's current UUID, so summoned, loaded, and
  spawned fish all carry (and fight with) exactly the traits their UUID says.
- **Abandoning a cast line = cutting it**: switching hotbar slots or stowing the rod
  while any line is out (fish or not) now gets the FULL cut-line treatment, identical to
  the keybind — the snip sound, the "Line cut." notice, and the re-rig rod cooldown.
  Deliberate reel-ins cost nothing.
- **A fish that snaps your line remembers it** (`fishAI.lineSnapShyTicks`, default 2
  minutes): after breaking free on a tension snap it shows no interest in ANY bobber —
  no approaching, no following, no biting — until the memory fades. You don't hook the
  same monster twice in a row by casting straight back out.
- **The Charge, redesigned around SLACK.** A charging fish swims toward you at a
  deliberate pace (about half its old rush — toward you, not into your boots), never
  charges when it's already near landing range, and the crank does not move it AT ALL.
  Instead the tension bar inverts its meaning: slack piles up every tick of the charge
  (`fight.chargeSlackTensionPerTick`) and cranking takes it back up
  (`fight.chargeCrankTensionRestore`, capped well below the snapping point — a slack
  line cannot be cranked into breaking). Let the line go FULLY limp and the fish can
  shake the unheld hook loose and escape (`fight.chargeHookThrowChance`). Snap the line
  at the top, lose the fish at the bottom — "reel fast, take up the slack!" is now
  literally the physics.
- **Darts never vanish unread anymore**: a dart could spring at the tail end of a calm
  phase and get cancelled seconds later by the next run starting — gone before the
  prompt was readable. Darts now only spring when the full reaction window fits in the
  phase's remaining time.
- **`/nicecatch summon` extreme weights are consistent now**: a 777-pounder needs a rare
  trait AND a top size roll, so the exact-match lottery sometimes came up empty ("No
  such individual exists") on pure luck. Requested traits stay a hard requirement, but
  weight is best-effort: if nothing lands inside tolerance, the closest-weighted
  candidate found is spawned (the summary shows its true weight). A shared time budget
  keeps 24-fish requests from stalling the server.
- **Supplemental fish spawning respects /gamerule doMobSpawning** — it's still mob
  spawning, so turning the rule off now stops Nice Catch's top-up schools too.
- **Fish trap overhaul.** Its own GUI (no more borrowed hopper row): the green-tinted
  bait slot stands alone on the left, an arrow points into the 2x2 haul grid — no
  guessing which slot does what. And the trap is bait-driven now: an UNBAITED trap
  catches nothing at all, plain food catches small fry only
  (`trap.trapFoodBaitMaxLbs`, 5 lbs), and putting a CAUGHT FISH in the bait slot raises
  the ceiling to several times the bait's own weight (`trap.trapBaitWeightMultiplier`,
  x8) — crumbs catch minnows, minnows catch keepers, keepers catch trophies. No more
  700 lb tuna strolling into an empty trap baited with nothing.
- **Carry fixes**: a truly giant fish (trophy tuna) no longer floats forward out of the
  hands (the size-scaled cradle offset is capped AND oversized fish are pulled back
  toward the chest proportionally), and only ONE heavy fish shows at a time — a second
  heavy fish in the other hand stays stowed instead of intersecting.
- **"Something bigger took your catch!" no longer smears over the fight hints** — the
  fight HUD draws its coaching at the vanilla actionbar height, so the chain-bite
  actionbar message overlapped it. The announcement now flashes in the fight HUD's own
  hint slot (top priority, 3 seconds, gold).
- **The bobber stays pinned to big fish' mouths**: the head-pin reach grew linearly with
  hitbox width, which on large fish overshot the model's actual snout and hung the bobber
  in open water ahead of the head. Reach growth is now sublinear — small fish unchanged,
  trophies keep the hook visually in the lip.
- **Fish health scales with size** (`weight.healthScaleExponent`, default 2.0): max
  health is multiplied by bodyScale^exponent, so a 500 lb leviathan tuna is a genuine
  tank while a minnow is fragile. Applied server-side, persists correctly, never stacks,
  and config changes take effect on load. 0 disables.

### Added (control & reach)
- **Scroll wheel can't lose the fight.** With a fish on the line the wheel never switches
  hotbar slots (even when not gripping the rod); the number keys still switch, deliberately.
- **Rebindable controls** (Options > Controls > Nice Catch): "Rod Control (hold)" — the
  grip/reel/charge hold, default right mouse — and "Cut the Line" (default V): give up on
  a fish that's too much, a bite, a retrieve, or a parked bobber. The line is snipped, the
  fish bolts, the bobber is lost, the rod takes no damage — but tying fresh tackle on
  takes a moment: the rod goes on a visible 10-second cooldown before it can cast again
  (`line.cutLineCooldownTicks`).
- **Longer line**: `line.rodRangeLimit` (default 64, vanilla hardcodes 32) is where the
  line now finally gives out; `lineLength` and `spoolLength` can be raised toward it for
  longer casts, fights, and boat trolling. NOTE: existing world configs keep their old
  values — raise `lineLength` in the world's serverconfig toml to feel it.

### Added (sculk ears)
- **Fish hear the world like sculk sensors** (`vibration` config section): fish now listen to the
  same vibration events sculk sensors and wardens do — footsteps, swimming, splashes,
  landing thumps, block breaking, bow shots, explosions — event-driven, so walking or
  swimming near a fish ALWAYS registers instead of sometimes slipping past the old
  positional checks. The sculk rulebook applies: sneaking is silent (crouched anglers stay
  invisible, in or out of the water), wool blocks the signal, and loud events (explosions,
  lightning, goat horns) carry 1.5x as far and cut through habituated calm. No sculk click,
  particle, or shriek is ever played — the fish just listen. Your own casting/reeling makes
  no noise, thrown FOOD keeps its gentler splash-then-attract chum behavior, fish don't
  hear each other, and land animals other than players are ambient noise. The old
  proximity checks still run underneath as a fallback for silent threats (drifting boats).

### Fixed
- **Darts can no longer be answered by the crank — at all.** Fast circling could still
  ratchet a PULL LEFT/RIGHT prompt complete before it was even readable (a circle sweeps
  through every direction, and the client-side gate leaked on fast spins). A dart is now a
  true interrupt, enforced server-side: the reel gains nothing while a dart is up, and the
  answering yank only counts once the crank has been still for a beat (~0.2 s) — stop
  reeling, read it, yank. The HUD prompt now says so ("STOP REELING - PULL LEFT!"), and
  hold-to-reel accessibility mode pauses its automatic crank during darts so those players
  can answer too.
- **A land-snagged fish slips the hook the moment it touches water.** Rescue-snagging a
  flopping fish and dragging it into water no longer keeps it on the line: it was never
  mouth-hooked (no bite), so at first water contact it comes free and bolts. Real
  bite-hooked fights are unaffected.

### Added
- **Individual fish sizes & the weight system**: every fish (modded included) rolls its own
  body scale — hitbox and model — from its UUID, so it needs no network sync, survives
  saves, and persists through catch-and-release. Small salmon are genuinely lighter, weaker
  fights; big ones heavier and harder (fight strength, net odds, and predator/prey checks
  all read the live hitbox). Weight = body volume x `weight.weightPerVolume`, shown on every
  caught fish's tooltip and in the on-screen catch line ("Caught Salmon — 4.2 kg Feisty").
  Config: `weight.sizeVariance*`. Note: fish items now carry weight NBT, so different
  individuals no longer stack.
- **Fish traits** (25 to start, trivially extensible — one register() line + two lang keys):
  a quarter of fish are born with a named quirk that changes how they behave and fight —
  Feisty (thrashes harder), Tough (strains the line), Mighty/Frail, Tireless/Lazy,
  Swift/Sluggish, Cunning (loves the charge), Slippery (shorter hook-set window),
  Greedy/Timid/Bold, Giant/Dwarf (size), Dense/Featherweight (heft), Lucky (bonus XP +
  double-catch) — plus rare aura fish that visibly shimmer in the water: Verdant,
  Glimmering, Molten, Ghostly, Abyssal, Cosmic, and Ancient. Traits are UUID-derived
  (deterministic, no sync), colored on the caught item's tooltip with a one-line
  description, and tuned via `weight.traitChance` / `doubleTraitChance` / `traitAurasEnabled`.
- **More fight sounds**: the hook setting home, line being stripped off the spool during a
  run, the rod creaking as tension nears the snap, and the line going sickeningly slack on
  an escape.
- **First-person rod animations** (`client.rodAnimationsEnabled`): the rod cocks back and
  trembles with the charge bar, whips forward on release, twitches while a fish worries the
  bait, and in the fight it bends with line tension, judders through runs, rises as you
  lift, and stirs in a small circle locked to your actual crank input.

### Fixed (AI stability)
- **Crowded fish settle down.** Fish now habituate: every scare stokes a panic fatigue that
  decays with quiet time — a fish spooked over and over runs shorter, shrugs off most
  further scares (even swimmers, once fully worn out), and stops extending its flight while
  a threat loiters nearby. A packed pond calms instead of thrashing itself frantic forever.
- **A fish attracted to your line stays on your line.** The attraction flag flickered with
  the bobber's own bobbing (isInWater blinks as the float pops above the surface), which
  made committed fish "snap" away to their school and back. Attraction now keys on the
  bobber's stable settled state, and a committed fish additionally holds its attention
  through brief wobbles (~1.5 s grace) — its focus breaks only for real reasons (scatter,
  hooked, the line leaves, or it drifts out of the leash).

### Added (compat & tuning)
- **Aquaculture fish mounts keep trait auras**: mount a Glimmering/Cosmic/Molten/etc. catch
  on a wall fish mount and it goes on shimmering with its trait's particles.
- **Per-species size bands**: fishProfiles entries now take `size_min`/`size_max`, replacing
  the global variance band for that species — used to let Aquaculture's weirdly tiny tuna
  grow to trophy size (default entry: 0.9-2.6x), and setting both to 1 opts a species out of
  size variance entirely.

### Added (the pond fights back)
- **Chain bites** (`fight.chainBiteChancePerSecond`, default 3%/s): while you reel a hooked
  fish in, a much larger fish nearby may strike it — your catch is eaten off the hook and
  the eater takes its place on the line. The small fish was the bait all along: the fight
  re-arms fresh against the monster (its strength, stamina, traits, full stamina, an
  opening run) with "Something bigger took your catch!" on screen. Once per fight.
- **Swimming fish can't be cast-snagged**: a thrown bobber no longer sticks to a fish in
  the water (vanilla sticks to anything it hits) — the only way onto a swimming fish's
  mouth is a bite it chose. A fish flopping on land is still fair game for a rescue-snag.
- **Darts can't be answered by accident**: circling sweeps through every direction, so
  crank motion no longer counts toward PULL LEFT/RIGHT — break the circle, then yank.

### Added (reel immersion wave)
- **Dart events** (`fight.dartEventsEnabled`): mid-fight the fish suddenly bolts sideways
  and the HUD demands "<<< PULL LEFT!" / "PULL RIGHT! >>>" — swing the mouse that way while
  holding right-click, inside the window (`dartWindowTicks`, `dartSwingRequired`). Answered:
  the fish is yanked back toward you and burns real stamina. Missed: it steals line and
  strains the rod. Darts strike often while it thrashes, occasionally from a dead hold, and
  a run cancels them.
- **Scroll-wheel reel control** (`client.scrollReelPerNotch`, 0 disables): scroll up adds
  crank, scroll down deliberately pays out line — big tension relief at the cost of the
  fish gaining ground. Hotbar switching is suppressed while working the reel.
- **Lift feedback**: a small gauge at the right end of the fight bar fills as rod-lift input
  registers, and the sounding/running hints turn green the moment your lift is landing — no
  more guessing whether the pull-up counted.
- **14 more traits** (39 total): Wild, Stubborn, Muscular, Sickly, Twitchy, Docile,
  Skittish, Voracious, Juvenile, Leviathan (very rare monster), and new auras — Iridescent
  (end rod), Stormborn (sparks, violent runs), Gilded (golden, pays), Cursed (smoke, brittle
  line, fearless).
- **Shallow-water spawning**: one-block-deep rivers now hold fish — small species only; big
  ones still need proper depth.
- `/nicecatch summon` accepts `random` for traits explicitly (weight 0 = random size), and
  `none` still forces a trait-free fish.

### Added (commands & multiplayer polish)
- **/nicecatch command suite** (op level 2). Type suggestions list ONLY the fish Nice
  Catch manages (no creepers or arrows in a fishing command), and non-fish types are
  rejected:
  - `summon <type> [count] [weightLbs] [traits]` — summon fish with a chosen weight and/or
    traits (comma-separated ids, or `none`). Sizes and traits are a pure function of the
    fish's UUID, so the command searches the lottery for a real matching individual and
    spawns exactly it — the result keeps its size and traits through saves and
    catch-and-release like any other fish. Spawns where you're aiming.
  - `give <type> [weightLbs] [traits]` — the caught item, stamped exactly as asked.
  - `inspect` — full report on the fish you're looking at: weight, scale, traits,
    interest, panic fatigue, hooked/fleeing flags.
  - `traits` — every registered trait with its color, id, rarity, and description.
  - `population [radius]` — per-species fish census around you.
  - `calm` / `scatter` / `chum` `[radius]` — clear fear and cooldowns, spook everything,
    or crank nearby fishes' bobber interest for testing.
- **Arrow-fight lines are visible to other players**: the line-arrow fight had no synced
  entity (a rod fight's bobber carries that for free), so only the angler ever saw the
  line. Arrow line state is now broadcast to everyone tracking the fish, and spectators
  render the angler-to-fish line; entries clean themselves up when either end disappears.

### Fixed (fish carry, third pass)
- Small fish now hang **vertically** (a rotation-order bug turned the nose-down pitch into
  a roll, leaving the fish horizontal), the third-person dangle renders at the hand instead
  of behind the player (its frame correction had a flipped sign), and the holding arm now
  extends forward to show the dangle off. The two-hand carry spreads the arms wider and
  levels the fish out of its downward slope (third person).
- **Aquaculture bait is trap bait**: worms, minnows, leeches and co. now work in the fish
  trap's bait slot — and as chum thrown straight into the water.

### Fixed (fish carry & trap, second pass)
- **Fish carry orientation**: the dangling fish now spins to face flank-out so its sway
  finally lines up with how a hanging fish flexes; in third person the entity gets a base
  frame correction (the third-person item socket is rotated and model-flipped — that was
  the "floating, detached" look).
- **True two-handed carry**: an 8+ lbs fish now poses BOTH arms forward (visible to other
  players — humanoid mobs and armor stands too), and the fish lays horizontally across the
  outstretched hands instead of reusing the dangle.
- **Fish shadows stay flat**: the swim-pitch/size render transform is now scoped to the
  model only, so the blob shadow no longer tilts with the fish.
- **Net cooldown means something**: it now applies only when the net comes up FULL
  (untangling the catch is the delay) — a miss costs nothing but the scattered spot it
  leaves — and it is enforced on the click-a-fish path, which previously bypassed it.
- **The fish trap has a real inventory**: right-click opens a hopper-style GUI — bait slot
  on the left (any food, shift-click to load), the four catch slots to its right. Old traps
  migrate their stored bait and haul automatically.

### Fixed (fairness & feel)
- **Far bites are winnable.** A fish hooked with almost no spool left used to open with a
  line-tearing run and escape near-instantly. It now opens thrashing in place and the
  escape-grace window stretches, so the angler can win line back before escape is possible.
- **Hooked players can escape**: a player on someone's hook now sees "You've been hooked!
  Hold sneak to break free!", sneaking mostly anchors them against the drag, and ~1.5 s of
  held sneak snaps the line (`line.playerEscapeCrouchTicks`, 0 restores old behavior).
- **Line pay-out is audible**: walking or boating away from a cast bobber clicks line off
  the reel as it pays out.
- **The net is a skill tool now**: odds collapse with weight (under 2 lbs full chance, 15+
  lbs x0.03 — trophies burst out of a hand net; hook-then-net cooperation stays the way to
  land them), a fish that sees the sweep coming mostly evades (approach from behind — sneak
  up), a bobber-distracted fish is easier, a fleeing fish nearly impossible. Cooldown 60.
- **Sneaking hides you from fish** on land entirely — the stealth approach for netting.
  A swimmer closing to half scare-range is an URGENT threat that cuts through even a
  habituated fish's calm: swim at fish and they bolt, always.
- **Bigger land animals resist the reel harder**: drag now falls on a steep bulk curve
  (chicken skids along, pig takes cranking, cow is a chore, horse barely notices).

### Performance
- Bobber searches no longer scan entity sections across the full world height per fish —
  live bobbers are tracked in a per-level registry, so the search is O(rods being fished)
  and free when no one is fishing. Threat scans run every 4th tick instead of every 2nd
  (reaction still under 0.2 s), and per-bobber claim counts are computed once per tick
  instead of once per asking fish. These were the paths that scaled with fish population.

### Added
- **Fish carrying, done properly** (`client.fishCarryEnabled`): a held fish item now renders
  as the actual fish ENTITY in the holder's hands — first AND third person, players, armor
  stands, even cod-carrying zombies — replacing the earlier first-person sprite pose. A
  light fish dangles head-down from the hand with a gentle sway; an 8+ lbs catch is laid
  across the arms; the model is scaled from the caught item's stamped weight, so a trophy
  looks like a trophy in someone else's hands. Fish items with no matching entity fall back
  to the normal item render.
- **Upgrade Aquatic support out of the box**: pike, lionfish, thrasher, and great thrasher
  join the default fishAiWhitelist (and the loner/boid blacklist); pike and the thrashers
  are profiled as predators, lionfish as a bold cover-lurker.
- **Aquaculture's double hook actually matters now**: its API reports a 10% double-catch —
  tuned for doubling whole loot hauls, invisible when each fight lands one fish — so it is
  scaled by `aquaculture.doubleHookChanceMultiplier` (default 3.5 → ~35%) and a proc is
  announced with a gold "x2!" on the catch line plus a pickup chime.
- **The reeled bobber respects terrain**: a retrieve that would drag the bobber into a
  block now climbs it up and over the lip instead (server and client agree), never phasing
  through solid ground.

### Changed
- **Weight-class endurance** (`fight.weightEndurance`): fights no longer end with big fish
  "giving up" early. A fish's weight now deepens its stamina reserves on a curve anchored in
  pounds — under 5 lbs tires quickly (the easy catch), 5-10 lbs fights up to ~2x as long,
  10-15 lbs up to ~3x (very difficult), and past 15 lbs it keeps climbing toward a 6x cap:
  an extreme, drawn-out battle where slack discipline and tension management actually
  matter. Heavyweights also get a strength floor that rises with the pounds, so a trophy
  fish never fights like a minnow regardless of the size curve.
- Fish weights display in **lbs by default** (`weight.weightUnit`, LBS or KG) — tooltips and
  the catch line both. Stored data stays metric, so flipping the unit never changes a fish.
- First-person rod animation directions corrected: the rod now rises with the charge bar
  (it dipped before), the cast whip snaps forward-and-down, line tension bends the rod down,
  and lifting raises it.
- **Controls tightened**: holding right-click alone no longer reels anything — circling the
  mouse is the reel, everywhere (fights already worked this way; plain retrieves now match).
  The old hold-to-reel behavior moved behind the accessibility option
  `client.requireCircularMotion = false`. The bite yank (hook-set) now also requires
  right-click held while jolting — no grip, no strike. Lifting the rod already required the
  grip (mouse input only reaches the reel while right-click is down).
- Catch-and-release now revives the exact fish: same size, same weight, same traits (the
  released fish keeps its identity instead of rolling a new one).
- **Species behavior profiles** (`species.fishProfiles`): every fish species now has a
  disposition — boldness, curiosity, bite bias, fight strength, stamina, food affinity,
  predator flag + prey size ratio, preferred depth, and cover (kelp/seagrass) affinity.
  Unlisted species get sensible size-based defaults (small fish bold/weak/quick to tire,
  big fish wary/strong/cover-loving); the config ships with tuned entries for vanilla fish
  and Unusual Fish's sharks.
- **Directional hook-set**: when a real fish bites, the HUD now tells you to **yank the rod
  LEFT or RIGHT** (a quick sideways mouse jolt) inside the bite window. The right way sets
  the hook; the wrong way (or dithering) loses the fish. Server-authoritative; toggle with
  `bite.directionalHookSet`, tune the required jolt with `client.hookSetJoltDegrees`.
- **Food attraction / chumming**: food items thrown into water attract fish. The fresh
  splash startles timid fish; bolder or hungrier ones swim over, circle, and eat it — which
  raises their bobber interest (chumming a spot genuinely improves the fishing). Config
  under `species.food*`.
- **Predation**: predator species (salmon and modded sharks by default) hunt fish much
  smaller than themselves — chase, gulp, satiation. Prey flees through the normal scatter
  system, so jinks and school panic come with it. Config under `species.predation*`.
- **Habitat idle movement**: fish now cruise on meandering headings, loiter, pause, drift
  between depth bands, and (big/cover species) lurk in kelp and seagrass, per species —
  replacing vanilla's pathfinding random-swim for managed fish (`species.habitatMovementEnabled`).
- **Snowy-biome fishing**: in water cold enough to snow, bite/interest rates are multiplied
  by `species.coldBiteMultiplier` (default 0.5) — slower, never dead.
- **Supplemental fish spawner**: when the water around a player runs light on fish, a fresh
  school (species and school size from the biome's own spawn list) appears 24+ blocks out.
  Own accounting — never touches vanilla mob caps or other mobs' spawning. Config under
  `spawning.extraSpawn*`; the school-spawn weight multiplier default also rose 1.0 → 2.5.
- **Fishing Net** (new item): sweep nearby water (or click a fish) to scoop one fish inside
  a small radius straight into your inventory — strong on small fish up close, weak on big
  ones, misses scatter the spot; cooldown + durability. **Cooperative**: netting a fish
  another player has hooked lands the catch for them instantly. Config under `net.*`.
- **Fish Trap** (new block): place underwater; it slowly catches fish that swim inside.
  Bait it with any food (right-click) to multiply the odds and actively draw fish over to
  investigate (via the same food-interest AI); each catch eats one bait. Right-click to
  collect; deliberately slow passive fishing. Config under `trap.*`.
- **Fish pitch**: fish visually tip their nose up/down with their vertical swimming,
  smoothed, client-side only (`client.fishPitchEnabled`).
- Fish fight tactics ("phases"): a hooked fish now cycles at random through five
  distinct behaviours during the fight, and the reel minigame changes with each one:
  - **Hold** — it sits still: your clean window to reel it straight in.
  - **Sweep** — it thrashes side to side: keep on it and reel; the thrashing fights
    your crank a little and the follow-camera swings with it.
  - **Pull** — it bores straight away and tears off line: you can't out-crank it, so
    hold on, keep some line, and let it wear itself out.
  - **Sound** — it dives for the bottom: pull the rod up (drag the mouse up) to lift it
    before it will come in; cranking alone barely moves a sounding fish.
  - **Charge** — it rushes back at you dumping slack: crank fast to take up the slack,
    or the fish gets to rest and recover its fight.
  - The next tactic is weighted by how worn out the fish is (fresh fish run, sound and
    charge often; tiring fish hold and thrash; a played-out fish stops running so it can
    be landed), and the same tactic rarely repeats twice in a row.
- Fight HUD hint now coaches the response to the fish's current tactic.
- **Gradual reel-in** for a line with no fish on it. Right-click no longer snaps the line
  straight back; instead **hold right-click and reel** (the same reel mode as a fight —
  camera locks to the bobber, slight zoom — but with no tension and no snapping):
  - **Empty line**: reel at full speed; circle the mouse to reel faster, or just hold for a
    steady baseline. Reel it to within a couple of blocks to retrieve it.
  - **Loot item** (the gold "something's on the line" bite): a little slower, but there's no
    fight and it can't slip off once you're reeling — reel it home to collect it.
  - Let go and the reel-in pauses (camera pans back); hold again to resume. So there are now
    three reel modes overall: nothing hooked (fastest), loot item (slower, no snap), and a
    real fish (the full fight).
  - Can be turned off with `line.gradualReelEnabled` for vanilla instant retrieval.
- **Spool line / boat trolling**: as you move away from a cast bobber the reel pays out line,
  and once you reach the end of the spool (`line.spoolLength`, ~30 blocks) the bobber is
  **dragged along behind you** instead of the line retracting when you get too far. Great for
  trolling a lure behind a moving boat. Toggle with `line.spoolDragEnabled`.
- **Fishing Line Arrow** (new item): an arrow with a fishing line attached, fired from any bow.
  - Craft it from an arrow + string (shapeless); found in the Combat creative tab.
  - Fire it at a fish and, if it hits, the reel fight begins for that fish with the line
    running from you to the fish — reel it in exactly like a rod fight (hold right-click and
    circle the mouse; your bow won't draw while you're reeling).
  - Fish caught with the line arrow are **not** added to catch-and-release: the item can't be
    tossed back into water to revive the fish.
  - Range and toggle via `lineArrow.arrowMaxLine` / `lineArrow.arrowFightEnabled`. Uses the
    vanilla arrow texture for now (a custom texture can be dropped in later).

### Fixed
- **Reeling a snagged entity on land finally works.** The gradual reel-in pulled the
  *bobber*, but a bobber stuck in an entity is re-pinned to that entity's position every
  tick (that's the hooked-entity glue), so nothing ever moved and the retrieve only
  "worked" at point-blank range. The reel now drags the **entity itself** toward you with
  real velocity — including on land, where a small hop breaks ground friction — scaled
  down by the entity's bulk, hard-capped so nothing is flung, finishing with vanilla's
  own final tug (`line.entityReelSpeed`).
- **True 3D reeling.** The fight winch now pulls the fish along the actual line direction:
  a fish below you is hauled upward, diagonal fish move along the diagonal, and a fish
  directly underneath rises instead of being ignored by the old horizontal-only pull.
- **Fights are winnable from above the water.** Landing (and the HUD bar) now uses an
  effective distance that forgives the height an elevated angler stands above the surface
  — fishing from a cliff, bridge, or boat deck no longer leaves the fish permanently
  outside landing range. Depth below the surface still counts in full, so a sounding fish
  must genuinely be lifted.
- The Fishing Line Arrow could not be fired from a bow (bows check the `minecraft:arrows`
  item tag, not the item class). The arrow is now in that tag, so any bow accepts it as ammo.
  Note: if you carry both regular and line arrows, the bow may grab the regular ones first —
  keep line arrows as your only arrows (or in the off-hand) to fire them.

### Changed
- The hooked bobber now **rolls so its hook clips up into the fish's mouth** (on top of the
  existing slide to the snout), for a proper "hooked in the mouth" look. Tunable/toggle via
  `client` config `bobberHookInMouth` / `bobberHookRotationDegrees` (180 = flip; 0 = off).
- Added a short **cooldown after a fight or reel-in finishes** before you can charge another
  cast, so a still-held right-click doesn't immediately fling the line back out
  (`client.castCooldownTicks`, default 12).
- Fish can now bite a **moving** bobber: fish lead a drifting bobber's motion and put on
  extra speed to keep pace with it, so a bobber pushed by a current, towed by a boat, or
  being reeled can still be approached and bitten. The bite-arrival range is a touch more
  forgiving so a moving bobber can still be reached at the moment of the bite.
- The bobber now sits in a hooked fish's **mouth**: it is pinned at the snout and slightly
  low, so the hook at the bottom of the bobber sinks into the front of the head and reads
  as a hook set in the mouth rather than a float hovering above the fish.
- The two-state "running / calm" fight was replaced by the five-tactic phase system above.
  Line snap, tension, fatigue, line length, escape grace and landing rules are unchanged.
- `fight.chargeChance` now weights how often the Charge tactic is chosen (previously the
  chance that a run was a charge). `fight.slackTakeFactor` is unused by the phase system
  and kept only for config compatibility.
- Fight strength now folds in the species' strength trait and a per-fish roll (±12%), so
  two same-size fish never fight identically; species stamina scales how fast fatigue
  builds. Existing `entityCatch.size*` configs still set the size curve underneath.
- A fish speared by the **fishing-line arrow** tires ~3× faster than a mouth-hooked one
  and never recovers stamina on slack — it still opens with a real fight but gives up much
  sooner (`fight.arrowFatigueMultiplier`).
- Species traits also color the wider AI: bold species approach bobbers sooner and spook
  less at a looming figure; curious species build interest faster; bite-prone species
  convert nibbles more often and win the "who bites" pick.
